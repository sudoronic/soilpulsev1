Plain-Language Earth Science & Soil Health Guide

Farmer-friendly explanations of satellite measurements, ground validation, and why diverse rotation crops build resilience.

Evapotranspiration (ET₀) & Water Balance

ET₀ is water evaporating from soil plus transpiring from leaves. Rainfall minus ET₀ shows whether your soil moisture bank is building or draining.

Soil Moisture Anomaly (SMAP)

Shows whether root-zone moisture is wetter or drier than normal for that week—spotting drought stress before planting or grain fill.

NDVI & Bare-Field Windows

NDVI measures living green cover (0.15 = bare dirt; 0.80 = lush crop). Low NDVI during May–June dust storms or monsoon rain signals topsoil erosion risk.

Frost Risk & Cold-Air Drainage

Low depressions in SRTM elevation collect cold night air. Knowing your frost-free window prevents planting sensitive legumes too early.

Satellite vs. Ground Measurements

Satellites track multi-year weather and regional water trends, while a farm rain gauge and soil lab test measure exact pH, SOM, and compaction.

Nitrogen-Fixing & Cover Crops

Legumes (mungbean, chickpea, soybean, berseem, clover) capture nitrogen from air (50–150+ kg N/ha), cutting urea bills and feeding microbes.

Deep-Rooting & Drought Crops

Sorghum, pearl millet, guar, and alfalfa send roots 1.5–2.5+ m deep—breaking hardpans and surviving heatwaves that stunt shallow grains.

Where Human Judgment Matters Most

Only you know local buyer contracts, equipment width, weed seedbanks, canal water turns, and cash-flow needs. Use SoilPulse to shortlist ideas—not dictate decisions.
